Daily ~20 min

  1. 1

    On Templates, sort by health and take the lowest one that earns. Today: Pixelfed 0%, Ubuntu desktop 33%, AzuraCast 38%, Firefox 44%.

    1. Check its upgrades first. Open Upgrades and find the template. If it’s behind, read the changelog for your symptom: an upstream fix may already exist.
    2. Find the root cause. Logs and memory on a fresh deploy. Railway can show SUCCESS while the app is dead inside the container (AzuraCast’s OOM loop).
    3. Ship one push. A patch or minor bump rides along with the fix. A major doesn’t: fix on the current version first, then do the major on its own, so a later drop points at one change.
    4. Canary, then main. Every push to main notifies all deployers. It’s the only way to reach the instances that are already broken.
  2. 2

    From the Candidates shortlist (5 now) or Search gaps (175, many of them noise). Judge an original on its own terms, not on kickback math.

  3. 3

    Origin feeds the verdict’s originals-vs-competitive split. From a terminal: dispatcherctl origin ID original. 9 templates have no kind yet.

Fix before you publish

When more than about 10 earning templates sit under 70%, skip step 2 that day. Every new template adds to the health and upgrade load for good.
